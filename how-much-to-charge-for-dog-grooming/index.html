How Much to Charge for Dog Grooming (Pricing by Size and Coat)

Updated November 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Two dogs of the same breed can take very different amounts of time on your table. A well-kept doodle on a four-week schedule is a pleasure; the same doodle after six months without a brush is a three-hour dematting job. Good grooming prices account for size, coat and condition, so the hard jobs pay what they should.

Start with your time

Grooming is priced by time, even when you quote a flat price. Work out what an hour on the table must earn to cover your pay, shampoo and supplies, blade sharpening, equipment, insurance, rent or van costs, and booking time. Then time your common grooms and build prices from there.

Price by size and coat type

Most groomers use a grid: dog size across, service down. Example ranges (adjust for your area; mobile groomers often charge more for convenience):

Service Small Medium Large Giant
Bath and brush (short coat) $35 to $55 $45 to $65 $55 to $80 $75 and up
Bath and tidy (trim face, feet, sanitary) $45 to $65 $55 to $80 $70 to $95 $90 and up
Full groom (haircut) $55 to $85 $70 to $100 $85 to $130 $110 and up

Doodles, double coats and other heavy coats are usually priced above the full groom row, because they take much longer to bathe, dry and brush out.

Charge for condition

Condition fees keep you from losing money on neglected coats, and they encourage regular booking:

  • Matting or dematting: charged by time, often per 15 minutes, with a note that severe matting may require a shave-down for the dog's comfort
  • Heavy shedding or de-shedding treatment
  • Handling fee for dogs that need extra time or a second person
  • Flea treatment if fleas are found, plus cleanup time

Explain condition fees at drop-off so nothing is a surprise at pickup.

Common add-ons

  • Nail trim and nail grinding
  • Teeth brushing
  • Ear cleaning
  • Anal gland expression (if you are trained to do it)
  • Blueberry or specialty facials
  • Paw balm and pad trimming
  • Bows, bandanas and cologne

Many groomers include nails and ears in a full groom and sell the rest as add-ons.

Reward regular clients

Dogs groomed every four to six weeks are faster and easier. A small discount for clients who pre-book their next appointment fills your calendar and keeps coats manageable.

Policies that protect your schedule

  • No-show and late cancellation fees
  • Late pickup fees, since your table and kennels are booked
  • Vaccination requirements, with proof on file
  • A signed grooming release covering matted coats, senior dogs and medical issues
