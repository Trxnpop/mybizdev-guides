How Much to Charge for Window Cleaning (Per Pane and Per Window)

Updated November 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Window cleaning is quoted by the window, not the hour, and the details matter. A single picture window, a divided-light French door and a third-story skylight are very different jobs. Here's how to build a price list that's quick to quote and fair for every home.

Count windows the same way every time

Most window cleaners price per window or per pane:

  • Per window: one price for a standard double-hung or slider, inside and out
  • Per pane: a price for each pane of glass, useful for French doors and divided-light windows

Decide how you count before you quote. For example, a double-hung window with two sashes might count as one window, while a French door with 15 small panes is priced per pane or as a "specialty" item.

Example residential rates

These illustrate the structure; local rates vary, so check competitors.

Item Example price
Standard window, outside only $4 to $8
Standard window, inside and outside $8 to $15
Large picture window $12 to $25
French door or divided panes Per pane, or a set price per door
Skylight $15 to $30, if safely accessible
Screen cleaning $2 to $5 per screen
Track and sill cleaning $2 to $5 per window

Adjust for height and difficulty

  • Second story: add a per-window amount for ladder work
  • Third story and up: higher, or quoted separately; consider water-fed poles
  • Hard water stains: priced separately with a note that heavy mineral deposits may not fully come off
  • Post-construction cleaning: paint, stickers and debris take much longer
  • Minimum charge: many cleaners set one so small jobs cover travel and setup

Offer recurring plans

Windows get dirty again, which is great for business. Offer a discount for quarterly or twice-yearly service, booked in advance. Recurring plans fill your calendar during slower months and build a predictable route.

Use a window count sheet

Walk around the home (or ask for photos) and count every window by type and floor: standard, large, French, skylight, plus screens and tracks. A window count sheet makes your quote accurate and shows the customer exactly what they're paying for.

Show the results

A quick before-and-after report with photos makes customers feel the value and gives you content for your Google profile and social media.
