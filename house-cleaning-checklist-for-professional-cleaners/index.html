Professional House Cleaning Checklist (Room by Room)

Updated November 12, 2026 · 3 min read

A written checklist is the difference between "pretty clean" and a clean the client notices. It keeps you from forgetting the same baseboard twice, makes your timing predictable so your prices stay profitable, and makes it possible to train a helper later. Here is a complete room-by-room checklist you can adapt for your business.

The golden rules of cleaning order

Professional cleaners move in a set pattern so they never clean the same spot twice.

  • Top to bottom. Dust ceiling fans and high shelves before counters, and counters before floors.
  • Dry to wet. Dust and vacuum before you mop or wipe with liquid.
  • Left to right (or clockwise). Work around each room in one direction so nothing gets skipped.
  • Let products sit. Spray the tub, toilet and stovetop first, then dust the room while the cleaner works.
  • Floors last, exit the room. Finish each room by mopping your way out the door.

Kitchen

Standard clean - Clear and wipe countertops and backsplash - Clean the exterior of appliances (fridge, dishwasher, oven, microwave) - Wipe inside the microwave - Clean the stovetop and knobs - Scrub and shine the sink and faucet - Wipe cabinet fronts where there are fingerprints - Empty trash and replace the liner - Vacuum and mop the floor

Deep clean adds - Wipe cabinet fronts fully and clean the handles - Degrease the range hood and filter - Clean baseboards and the toe kick under cabinets - Inside the oven and inside the fridge (often priced as add-ons) - Move small appliances and wipe underneath

Bathrooms

Standard clean - Spray the tub, shower and toilet first and let them sit - Clean mirrors and glass - Wipe counters, the sink and faucet - Scrub the tub and shower walls - Clean the toilet inside and out, including the base and behind it - Wipe towel bars, the toilet paper holder and light switches - Empty trash - Vacuum and mop the floor

Deep clean adds - Scrub grout lines - Remove soap scum and hard water buildup from glass doors - Wipe cabinet fronts and baseboards - Clean the exhaust fan cover - Wipe the door, door frame and handle

Bedrooms

Standard clean - Make the bed or change linens if requested - Dust surfaces, nightstands and lamps - Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures you can reach safely - Clean mirrors - Empty trash - Vacuum carpet or vacuum and mop hard floors, including under the bed edges

Deep clean adds - Dust blinds and window sills - Wipe baseboards, doors and light switches - Vacuum under furniture where possible

Living areas

Standard clean - Straighten pillows and blankets - Dust shelves, the TV stand and decor - Wipe the coffee table and side tables - Vacuum upholstery if requested - Vacuum and mop floors

Deep clean adds - Dust blinds, sills and vents - Wipe baseboards and door frames - Vacuum under cushions

Whole-home finishing touches

  • Wipe light switches and door handles
  • Check every room for missed trash
  • Take a final walk through the home with fresh eyes
  • Lock up and set the alarm exactly as instructed

How to use a checklist with clients

A checklist is also a sales and communication tool.

  1. Show it during the walkthrough. Clients understand the difference between a standard and a deep clean when they see the list.
  2. Mark priorities. Ask the client to circle the three things they care about most. Do those perfectly every time.
  3. Note exclusions. If you do not do inside windows or laundry, say so on the checklist so nobody is surprised.
  4. Leave it behind. A checked-off list left on the counter shows the client exactly what you did.

Time each room to protect your prices

Use the checklist to time yourself for a few weeks. You will learn that a typical bathroom takes you 20 to 30 minutes and a kitchen 30 to 45. Those numbers let you quote new homes accurately and spot when a client's home is taking longer than you are being paid for.
