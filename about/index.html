About MyBizDev

MyBizDev makes printable forms and spreadsheets for people starting small service businesses: house and Airbnb cleaners, carpet and window cleaners, pressure washers, painters, handymen, lawn care crews, mobile detailers, pet sitters, dog groomers, lash artists and nail techs.

The guides on this site cover the questions new owners ask first: what to charge, what paperwork you need, what goes in a contract, and how to get the first few clients. Every guide stands on its own, and you don't need to buy anything to use it.

When a guide mentions a template, it links to the matching kit in our Etsy shop. Those are our own products.

Nothing here is legal, tax or insurance advice. Rules differ by state, county and city, so check with your local licensing office and an insurance agent before you take on clients.
