How Much to Charge for Carpet Cleaning: Per Room vs Per Square Foot

Updated November 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Carpet cleaning prices confuse customers because every company quotes differently. Some advertise a low "per room" price and add fees on site; others quote by square foot. Pick a method that is simple to explain and covers your costs, and stick to it.

The two main pricing methods

Method How it works Pros Cons
Per room A flat price per standard room (often up to a size limit, such as 200 to 300 sq ft) Easy to quote on the phone Big rooms and open floor plans need extra rules
Per square foot A rate per square foot of carpet Fair and accurate Customers can't compare it easily

Many cleaners quote per room for homes and per square foot for large areas and commercial jobs.

Example residential rates

These show the structure, not a quote for your area. Call or check a few local competitors.

  • Per room: commonly $30 to $75 per standard room, with a size limit
  • Per square foot: commonly $0.25 to $0.75 per sq ft, depending on method and area
  • Stairs: $2 to $5 per step, or a flat price per staircase
  • Hallways and closets: often small flat fees, or free with a set number of rooms
  • Minimum charge: $100 to $175 is common, so small jobs still cover travel and setup

Upholstery and extras

Upholstery is usually priced per piece:

  • Sofa: often $75 to $150
  • Loveseat: often $60 to $120
  • Armchair or recliner: often $40 to $80
  • Dining chairs: per seat
  • Mattresses and area rugs: per piece or per sq ft

Common add-ons:

  • Pre-treatment for heavy traffic areas
  • Spot and stain treatment (pet urine, wine, coffee); be clear that some stains may not come out
  • Pet odor treatment
  • Deodorizer and carpet protector
  • Moving furniture (many cleaners move small items only)

Why a pre-inspection protects you

Before you start, walk the job with the customer and note existing damage, stains that may be permanent, loose seams and color loss. Have them sign a pre-inspection release. It sets expectations and protects you from being blamed for problems that were already there.

Avoid the "bait and switch" reputation

Low advertised prices followed by surprise fees get bad reviews. Instead, publish a clear price list, quote the full price before you start, and get approval in writing for anything extra.

Price repeat customers

Carpet cleaning is often a once- or twice-a-year job. Offer a small repeat-customer discount and send a reminder six or twelve months later. Pet owners and families with kids often book more frequently.
