Lash Extension Aftercare Instructions to Give Every Client

Updated November 12, 2026 · 3 min read

Great retention starts after the client leaves your chair. Most "my lashes fell out" complaints come down to aftercare, so the more clearly you explain it, the happier your clients and the better your reviews. Here's a complete set of aftercare instructions you can adapt and hand to clients.

Adjust these to match your adhesive manufacturer's guidance and your own training.

The first 24 to 48 hours

Adhesive keeps curing after the appointment. During this window, ask clients to:

  • Keep lashes dry: no washing the face directly over the eyes, no swimming, no steam rooms or saunas
  • Avoid hot showers aimed at the face; keep steam to a minimum
  • Skip mascara and eye makeup near the lash line
  • Avoid rubbing or touching the lashes

Some modern adhesives cure faster, so follow what your product recommends and tell the client the exact time frame.

Daily care

Clean lashes last longer and help prevent irritation:

  • Cleanse daily with an oil-free lash cleanser or foam and a soft brush
  • Rinse gently with water and pat dry with a lint-free towel
  • Brush through lashes with a clean spoolie each morning once dry
  • Sleep on your back or use a silk pillowcase to reduce friction

What to avoid

  • Oil-based products near the eyes, including makeup removers, cleansers and creams; oil can break down adhesive
  • Mechanical lash curlers
  • Waterproof mascara and any mascara on the extensions
  • Picking or pulling at lashes, even loose ones; it can damage natural lashes
  • Rubbing eyes hard when removing makeup

Normal shedding vs a problem

Natural lashes shed a few a day as part of their growth cycle, and extensions come off with them. Losing a few extensions a day is normal. Signs to contact the studio right away:

  • Itching, burning, swelling or redness that doesn't settle
  • Watery or irritated eyes lasting more than a day
  • Any sign of infection

Clients with a reaction should contact the artist and seek medical advice. Removal should be done by a professional, not at home.

When to book a fill

Most clients need a fill every 2 to 3 weeks to keep lashes full. Many studios require a minimum percentage of extensions remaining for it to count as a fill rather than a new full set. Put that rule on the aftercare card so it isn't a surprise at checkout.

Lash lift and brow lamination aftercare

For lifts and lamination, the main rules are similar but shorter:

  • Keep the area dry for 24 hours
  • Avoid steam, saunas and heavy sweating for 24 hours
  • No mascara or brow products for 24 hours
  • Brush brows into place daily; consider a nourishing oil after the first day if your aftercare allows it

How to hand out aftercare

Verbal instructions get forgotten by the time the client reaches the car. Give a printed aftercare card at checkout and text a copy the same day. It reduces "is this normal?" messages and makes your studio look polished.
